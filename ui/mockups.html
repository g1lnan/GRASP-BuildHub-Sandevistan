Every reward is earned by showing up, never by scoring well.

Phiên vấn đáp

Bright palette and chunky buttons — but no confetti, no mascot, no bouncing timer. A student mid-thought under a running clock does not need a dancing buffalo. The reward comes after the thinking.

680px desktop · identical stack on mobile
Equal weight is a hard requirement.
Voice and typed are the same 3D button, the same size, the same scoring. A student who never speaks must not be able to tell they took a different path. (FR-302, NFR-8)
The timer never turns red.
Game energy lives in the buttons and the celebration — never in time pressure. There is no red token anywhere in this design system.
Progress bar replaces v1's hidden turn count.
Duolingo's motivational win beats the mild framing concern. The bar shows how far through you are — not what's coming next.

The score sits below the celebration, not above it.
A student who tried and scored 2.1 still earned 80 XP, extended a 12-day streak, and unlocked a concept. That is the learning-first thesis rendered in UI.
❤️ Hearts are the one Duolingo mechanic we drop.
Running out of hearts in Duolingo ends a free practice session — reload tomorrow, nothing lost. Running out mid-defense would end a graded assessment. There is no defensible version of "you failed your coursework because you used five hearts." Every other mechanic survives intact.
Nghé is never disappointed.
A baby water buffalo — trâu, the Vietnamese icon of diligence (chăm chỉ như trâu). Culturally specific rather than another owl. Nghé has an encourage state but never a sad or disapproving one: an accusing mascot is still an accusation.

What is earned, and how

The single hard rule: every visible reward comes from showing up and trying, never from scoring highly. That is what makes gamification safe on a graded assessment.

★ XP — Điểm KN
+50 a defense · +10 a follow-up · +20 same-day. Never for scoring well
🔥 Streak — Chuỗi ngày
Any defense on a given day. Never broken by a low score
◈ Concept Map
Unlocks at D2 ≥ 3.5. Never locked or removed once earned
✦ Badges — Huy hiệu
Milestones. Never for outscoring a peer
▲ League — Giải đấu
Weekly XP among ~20 anonymised peers. Never ranked on understanding
🐃 Nghé
idle · thinking · cheer · encourage · sleep. Never disappointed
